# Holi Labs

Practical tutorials, field notes, experiments, and reviewed technical papers for Holi.tools.

- URL: https://labs.holi.tools/
- App path: `web/apps/labs`
- Content: `web/apps/labs/src/content`
- English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Bengali, and Portuguese are complete public content locales.
- Papers is a format inside Labs, not a separate product.
- Use Labs for education and explanations; use `spec/` for normative requirements and
  `docs/decisions/` for durable architecture choices.
